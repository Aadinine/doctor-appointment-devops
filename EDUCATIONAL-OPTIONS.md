# Educational Cloud Access Options

## Student Benefits
Many cloud providers offer free credits to students:

### 1. Azure for Students
- Go to https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/free/students/
- Use your school email (.edu, .ac.in, etc.)
- $100 credit + free services
- No credit card required with student verification

### 2. GitHub Student Developer Pack
- Go to https://education.github.com/pack
- Includes Azure credits
- DigitalOcean credits
- Cloudinary credits
- Many other services

### 3. AWS Educate
- https://aws.amazon.com/education/educate/
- Free credits for students
- No credit card required

## How to Apply
1. Use your college email address
2. Provide student ID verification
3. Get instant access to cloud credits

## If You Don't Have Student Email
- Check if your college provides cloud access
- Ask your computer science department
- Some colleges have institutional cloud accounts
